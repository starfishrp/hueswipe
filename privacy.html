Rules & privacy

Last updated: November 2026. Hue Swipe is a small demo project.

The short version

Hue Swipe stores only what it needs to work: your email, your @, and the colours, captions, comments and likes you post. There are no ads, nothing is sold, and you can delete everything at any time from Me → Delete my account.

Who can use it

You need to be 16 or older to make an account.

Rules

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying or hate speech.
  • No spam, scams or impersonating other people.
  • Keep captions and comments suitable for everyone.

Posts, comments or accounts that break these rules may be removed. You can report anything from its ⋯ menu, and block anyone from their profile.

What’s stored

  • Your account: email address and password. Passwords are stored hashed by our database provider and can’t be read by anyone.
  • Your profile: your @username, which other signed-in users can search for.
  • What you post: colours, captions, comments, likes, friend requests, blocks and reports.
  • On your device: your login session and a few settings (like your Doomscroll colours), kept in your browser’s local storage. No tracking cookies.

Who can see it

  • Your posts, and the likes and comments on them, are visible only to you and your friends.
  • Your @username is visible to other signed-in users so friends can find you.
  • Who you’ve blocked and anything you report are private.
  • The person running this demo can see the database to keep it running and deal with reports.

Where it’s kept

Data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database service. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

Go to Me → Delete my account. Your account and everything you’ve posted are removed straight away.

It’s a demo

Hue Swipe is provided as-is, and features may change or the demo may be reset.
